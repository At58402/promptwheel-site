PromptWheel

Support

Your saved prompts on a wheel, one shortcut away. Press the shortcut in any text field, pick a prompt, and it's pasted in for you.

Getting started

  1. Open PromptWheel. It lives in your menu bar.
  2. Allow it to paste: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Device Control and Data Access (called Accessibility on macOS 26), then turn on PromptWheel. If it isn't listed, click + below the list and choose PromptWheel from Applications. The welcome window has a button that takes you there.
  3. Click into any text field, press ⌃Space, and pick a prompt.

Using the wheel

  • Point and click a prompt, or press its assigned key.
  • Hold the shortcut, point at a prompt and let go to paste it in one motion.
  • Scroll to spin through prompts, then click or press Return.
  • Pinch in on a trackpad, or press Esc, to close the wheel.
  • Press , or click the gear at the bottom to open Settings.

Common questions

The wheel opens but nothing is pasted.

PromptWheel needs permission to paste. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Device Control and Data Access (called Accessibility on macOS 26), make sure PromptWheel is turned on. If it already is, turn it off and on again.

It says "Secure input is on".

Password fields, and Terminal when Secure Keyboard Entry is on, block apps from typing for you. That's a macOS security feature. PromptWheel copies the prompt instead, so press ⌘V to paste it.

Does it press Enter or send my message?

No. PromptWheel only pastes. You always decide when to send.

Can I fill in text automatically?

Yes. Add {clipboard} to a prompt to insert whatever you last copied, or {date} and {time} for the current date and time.

How do I change the shortcut?

Open Settings, click the shortcut in the toolbar, and press the new key combination.

How do I move my prompts to another Mac?

Turn on Sync prompts with iCloud in Settings → General, or use Export and Import.

PromptWheel

Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

PromptWheel does not collect, store or share any of your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to our servers. We don't have any.

Your prompts

The prompts, labels, keys and shortcut you set up are stored only on your Mac, inside PromptWheel's own app storage. They never leave your Mac unless you choose to:

  • Turn on iCloud sync. Your prompts are then stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they appear on your other Macs. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't see or access it.
  • Export them. Export saves a file wherever you choose. What happens to that file is up to you.

Accessibility permission

PromptWheel asks for macOS Accessibility permission for one purpose: to paste the prompt you pick into the text field you're using, by sending the standard ⌘V paste command. It does not read, record or monitor what you type, what's on your screen, or the contents of other apps.

Clipboard

To paste, PromptWheel briefly places your prompt on the clipboard, then puts back what was there before. It reads the clipboard's contents only when a prompt contains the {clipboard} placeholder, to insert that text into the prompt you're pasting. Nothing from the clipboard is stored or sent anywhere.

Keyboard shortcut

PromptWheel registers a single keyboard shortcut with macOS (⌃Space by default) and is notified only when that shortcut is pressed. It does not see any other keys you press, except keys pressed while the wheel itself is open.

Children

PromptWheel collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because PromptWheel collects no data, any change would only make that clearer.
